      Evidence model

      Four layers, each with a different job.

      The checker separates evidence preservation from system availability and business usefulness. Passing one layer never stands in for another.

      01

      Capture

      Keep the authoritative response, retrieval time, source URL, and enough context to interpret it.

      02

      Integrity

      Rebuild row and batch seals from stored values, including database type-coercion edge cases.

      03

      Operations

      Measure freshness against the promised cadence. Unreadable or unreachable inputs resolve to UNKNOWN.

      04

      Decision

      Name the person, decision, and artifact that consumes the output. An unread corpus is not a product.
